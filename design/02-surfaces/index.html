Surfaces

SurfacesStackRailTable

The six surfaces at rest, without navigation. The prototypes are Stack, Rail and Table; add #tour to any of them to watch it play. Each navigation direction arranges these same surfaces, so the directions differ only in where things live and how you move between them. Two are homes on the ground: Attention for deciding and Overview for overseeing, the two views in VISION.md. The other four open as sheets on top of them.

Attention

The deciding home. One column, read top to bottom: urgent first, then by subject. The first decision shows its options in place.

Below the line are things set aside, as a count, and recent decisions that fade with age before moving to the archive.

Overview

The overseeing home. Missions in motion, the changelog and the agents. "Unasked" marks the fixes agents made without asking, which VISION.md wants visible.

Nothing here asks anything of you. That's the attention home's job.

Mission

The step track from the mission card, stood up as a vertical path. The current step opens to show what's happening. Below the path is what the mission has made so far.

Conversation

With the chief of staff, on the bare table. On the right is what became of what you said: each idea as an item, with where it is now. With a sheet open, the conversation docks beside it instead.

This is how you can see that talking turned into something.

Thinking space

Deep focus on one question. The researcher's sheets are laid out on open ground: the comparison, both renderers side by side, and a benchmark. The researcher is docked at the seam on the right.

One text button, because choosing is the point of this surface.

Find

Find anything. One field, with results grouped by concept, and each result drawn in its own shape: a decision as a record, a mission as its card, a change as a changelog row.

The mission called this view Explore. It turned out to be a verb, not a place: find works on everything and can be called from anywhere.
