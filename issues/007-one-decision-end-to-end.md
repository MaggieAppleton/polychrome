# Run one decision through the whole loop by hand

Status: idea

Before designing the attention queue, take one small decision ("which Markdown renderer?") through every stage:

1. It's logged as an issue.
2. A prepare mission researches it and builds a comparison.
3. I decide from that comparison.
4. The decision is recorded.
5. It becomes a mission, which ends with the quality mission.

Note what each stage needed from the interface. That tells us what the queue and the deciding view must show.
