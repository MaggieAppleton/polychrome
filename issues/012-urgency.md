# Decide what's urgent enough to break through focus

Status: idea

In deep focus, most attention waits until I step back. Only urgent things should reach me, as the slip at the bottom edge (`design/01-grammar/layers.html`). An agent's ordinary question shouldn't interrupt me while I'm working on another decision.

Two kinds of thing could count as urgent:

- an urgent problem
- an agent that has been stuck on me for a long time and needs to get moving again

Open questions:

- How is urgency calculated? Is it a score, rules, or a judgement by the chief of staff?
- What do we call it?
- Who sets it: the agent raising the item, Polychrome, or both?
- How long is "stuck for a long time"? Does it depend on what's waiting behind the item?
